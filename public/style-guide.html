A collector’s working catalog: precise records, readable controls and clear ownership.

Type, spacing and controls

16px body and inputs · 14px labels · 28px dossier titles · 21px section titles.

16px field gaps · 24px section spacing · 44px controls · 6px control corners · 10px panel corners.

Keyboard focus has a brass outline. Disabled controls use a not-allowed cursor; loading has explicit status text.

Release names span two columns. Notes span the full row. Accessory lists use two columns on wide screens; save and cancel remain visible.

Photos and editing

Archive photos and your own collection photos have separate labels. Choose a local image through a visible file selector, or use the camera with an explicit preview and capture action.

Only the owner can edit. A locked form explains its sign-in status and offers a sign-in action. Uploads support JPG, PNG and WebP up to 8 MB; camera errors explain the local-file option.
