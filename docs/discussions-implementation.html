Discussion implementation

G.I. Jeff · October 9, 2026 · Requirements · Migration plan

Delivered scope

  • Signed-in-only discussion feed and titled threads. Site topics and release-specific topics share one system; an item thread retains its release link in the site-wide feed.
  • Immediate plain-text posting, chronological replies, 24-thread and 50-post pages, title search, follow/unfollow, and durable in-app reply notifications with read state.
  • Thread creators automatically follow their thread. Other collectors choose Follow replies. Posting does not subscribe a collector automatically.
  • Author edits use version checks, an edited marker, and administrator-visible revision history. Author removal clears text and revisions while preserving a conversation placeholder.
  • Reports are visible only to the owner administrator. Moderation supports hide/restore, lock/unlock, title correction, report resolution, and discussion posting restrictions with a reason and audit trail.
  • Restricted collectors can read discussions and use their collections, but cannot create/reply/edit/remove/report. The owner administrator cannot be restricted. Removed posts cannot be restored.
  • Account deletion removes authored discussion text, revisions, thread titles, reports submitted by the collector, follows, notifications, and restrictions. The thread/post structure remains under Deleted collector. Reports and moderation reasons concerning deleted authored content are redacted. Deletion-ledger replay repeats this cleanup after a database restore.

Plain text is rendered through React text nodes; there is no HTML/Markdown execution, discussion attachment upload, private messaging, or payment functionality. Text copied into another collector's own post is not automatically attributed or scrubbed; reports and owner moderation remain available for that content.

Where to use it

  • /discussions: site-wide feed and new site topic.
  • /discussions?release=…: all threads scoped to one release and a new item topic. Release details expose Discuss this release.
  • /discussions/[thread]: posts, follow control, replies, author editing and owner moderation.
  • /notifications: the latest 50 reply events and mark-read controls. Events include thread context, not copied post bodies or email addresses. Mark all read also covers older events.
  • /discussions/moderation: open reports, posting restrictions and the latest 50 moderation actions. Full audit records persist in D1.

Discussions and Notifications links are available in the signed-in catalog header and shared page navigation. Hosting remains owner-only until the separate public-release gates are satisfied.

Storage and request contracts

Migration 0005_bent_jackal.sql adds eight normalized discussion tables, foreign keys, indexes, uniqueness for reports and notifications, and post-state checks. Existing migrations remain unchanged. New content lives in D1; no new R2 objects are required.

All discussion reads authenticate the collector. All writes enforce same-origin requests, ownership or administrator role, and durable rate limits. Public responses omit login email, private collection fields, report details and revision bodies. Moderation and author edits use optimistic versions. Lock/restriction checks guard the write itself, preventing an in-flight reply from bypassing a lock. Private responses use no-store caching.

Titles are limited to 160 characters, post bodies to 6,000, report/moderation reasons to 1,000, and JSON bodies to 32 KiB. Limits: 10 new threads/hour, 60 post changes/hour, 10 reports/hour, 60 follow changes/hour, and 60 moderator/notification writes/minute. Up to 200 explicitly followed threads per collector; thread creation also creates an automatic follow. Notifications are deduplicated by recipient/post. Client request IDs prevent duplicate thread/reply creation on retries.
